Privacy Policy

Jacklinus is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our encrypted chat platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Display Name: When you create or join a chat room, you may provide a display name to identify yourself in conversations.
  • Chat Messages: Any text messages you send through our platform.
  • Shared Photos: Images you upload and share in chat rooms.
  • Drawings: Any drawings or annotations you create using our collaborative drawing feature.

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data: Basic information about how you interact with our platform, including connection times and feature usage.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device type, and other technical identifiers necessary for service functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Services: To enable real-time encrypted chat, drawing collaboration, and file sharing between users.
  • Improve User Experience: To understand how users interact with our platform and make improvements.
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access, maintain platform integrity, and prevent abuse.
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries and provide customer support.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Encryption: All chat messages and shared content are encrypted during transmission. We utilize industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your data.

3.2 Temporary Storage: Chat rooms and their associated content are temporary by design. Data is stored only as long as necessary to maintain active chat sessions.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only:

  • When required by law or to comply with legal processes
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • With service providers who assist in platform operations (subject to confidentiality obligations)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Opt-out of non-essential data collection
  • Terminate your session at any time using the "Terminate Session" feature

6. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.
